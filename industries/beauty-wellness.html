Frequently Asked

Common questions from salons and studios

How do salons reduce client no-shows?

Most no-shows are forgetting, not flaking. An automatic reminder the day before an appointment, and again a couple hours out, catches the majority of them. Making it just as easy to reschedule as to no-show also helps, since a client who has to call to change a time is more likely to just skip it.

How do I get more clients to rebook after their appointment?

Rebooking works best when it happens automatically, not when it depends on remembering to ask at checkout. A follow-up message a few weeks after the appointment, timed to when that client typically needs to come back in, brings people back without you having to chase them down.

What's the best way to manage bookings for a small salon or studio?

Put every booking source, phone, walk-in, online, into one shared calendar so nothing double-books. Layer automatic reminders and confirmations on top. Most salons don't need complicated booking software, they need the tools they already have connected to one clear schedule.
